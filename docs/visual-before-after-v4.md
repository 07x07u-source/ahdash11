# Visual Before / After V4

| Surface | Before | V4 |
|---|---|---|
| Login | Three equal, heavy panels | Full-bleed football artwork, story side, compact action side, inline error |
| Home | One Party action plus utility links | Two clear product actions: Party and Tournament, with small resumable context |
| Profile | Three-column dashboard with XP/rating/cards/shortcut grid | Player-led football identity, one inline stats rail, club/league/category story |
| Tournament | No product surface | Creation, teams, draw, round paging, match launch, final, champion, history |
| Typography | Tajawal body + Noto Kufi display | Thmanyah Sans throughout; Serif reserved for editorial/champion moments |
| Radius | Soft oversized card language | Tighter 8/12/18/22 radius scale with clearer hierarchy |

V4 preserves the charcoal/off-white/lime/gold identity, SafeArea behavior, RTL, dark/light themes, and 800×360 through 1366×768 landscape targets. Tournament golden coverage contains 80 images: eight states × two themes × five sizes. Party and existing visual suites remain regression coverage for the unchanged engine surfaces.
